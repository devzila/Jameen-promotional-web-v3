FAQ

Questions committees usually ask.

What happens if my subscription expires?
Your data stays safe. The society is moved to Free Community limits (up to 20 units). You can view existing records; adding new ones resumes when you renew or upgrade.
Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime?
Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades apply at the end of your current billing cycle so you keep paid features through the period you've already paid for.
Is society data secure?
Yes. Access is role-based for committee members and staff. Financial and resident data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Only authorised roles in your society can see sensitive records.
Can I export my data?
CSV export is available on Growing, Large, and Enterprise plans. Free and Small plans let you view data on the dashboard; bulk export unlocks with Growing and above.
Can we migrate from Excel or another platform?
Yes. We help import flats, residents, and opening balances. Larger societies and portfolios get hands-on migration support on Enterprise — and founding societies get setup help included.
Why choose annual billing?
Annual plans include roughly two months free versus paying month to month, fewer renewals for the treasurer, and priority onboarding when slots are limited.
