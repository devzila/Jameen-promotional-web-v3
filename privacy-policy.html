Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Jameen (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, stores and shares information when you use the Jameen Resident mobile application on iOS and Android (package / bundle ID app.jameen.resident).

Effective date: 25 September 2026 Last updated: 25 September 2026 Applies to: Jameen Resident App

1. Who we are

Jameen provides housing-society and community operating software, including the Resident mobile app used by residents of societies that subscribe to the Jameen platform.

Your society (or its management company) is typically the organisation that creates your resident account and decides which community services are available. Jameen operates the technology platform that stores and processes that information on their behalf and ours.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy applies to:

  • The Jameen Resident app for iOS and Android
  • Related resident authentication, profile, billing views, helpdesk, visitor pre-approvals, announcements and in-app notifications delivered through that app
  • Communications we send about your account or the app (for example password-reset emails)

It does not replace your society’s own rules, bye-laws or notices. Where society administrators enter or manage data about you, they remain responsible for how that operational data is used inside their community.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Account & profile information

  • Authentication credentials (passwords are stored hashed; we do not store plaintext passwords)
  • Profile details associated with your resident membership, including optional profile photo
  • Society / community association (company slug), unit(s) linked to you, and related membership status

3.2 Community & operational information

Depending on features enabled by your society, the app may display or let you submit:

  • Maintenance and helpdesk requests, comments, status history and attached photos
  • Visitor / guest pre-registrations and visit history for your unit
  • Invoices, dues, payment history and invoice PDFs (view / download)
  • Announcements, notices and related comments
  • Vehicles and other household details registered by the society office
  • Dashboard summaries related to your unit and open requests

3.3 Device & technical information

  • Device identifiers needed for secure sessions and push delivery (for example push tokens when notifications are enabled)
  • App version, operating system and basic diagnostics needed to operate and improve the service
  • IP address and standard server logs when the app communicates with Jameen APIs
  • Crash or error reports if diagnostic tooling is enabled in a given build

3.4 Information we do not sell

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use resident app data to serve third-party advertising inside the Resident app.

4. Device permissions

The Resident app may request the following permissions. You can deny or later revoke them in system settings; some features will then be unavailable.

  • Camera — to capture or update your profile photo
  • Photo library — to choose a profile photo, and to save invoice PDFs you choose to export or share
  • Notifications — to deliver alerts about visitors, maintenance updates, notices and related community events (when enabled by your society and permitted by you)

The app does not require continuous background location access to use core resident features.

5. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Authenticate you and keep your session secure
  • Provide resident features: profile, dues visibility, helpdesk, visitors, announcements and related workflows
  • Notify you about activity relevant to your unit or account
  • Support password resets and account recovery
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, debug issues and improve reliability
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms

6. How we share information

We may share information with:

  • Your society / management company — authorised committee members, managers and staff who need the information to operate the community (for example to process a complaint or verify a visitor)
  • Service providers — infrastructure, email delivery, analytics or push notification providers who process data on our instructions
  • Payment gateways — if online resident payments are enabled for your society in the future; card or UPI details are handled by the gateway under their terms (the current Resident app primarily displays dues and history managed by your society)
  • Legal / safety — when required by law, regulation, court order, or to protect rights, safety and integrity of users and the platform

Other residents do not receive your private account credentials. Visibility of community content (for example notices) follows your society’s configuration and access roles.

7. Storage, security & retention

  • Data is transmitted using encrypted HTTPS connections
  • Access tokens and similar secrets on device are stored using the platform secure storage mechanisms provided by the app framework
  • Server-side data is protected with access controls, tenant isolation between organisations, and operational safeguards described on our Trust & Security page
  • We retain information for as long as your account remains active with a society on Jameen, and thereafter as needed for backups, disputes, security logs and legal requirements

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We work to protect your information, but we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices & rights

Depending on applicable law and your society’s processes, you may:

  • Review and update certain profile details in the app
  • Change your password
  • Turn off push notifications in device settings
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • Request deletion or export of personal data, subject to society records requirements and legal retention needs

For account closure or data deletion that is held as part of society records (for example historical invoices), contact your society administrator as well as us. We will coordinate where we are the processor of that data.

9. Children

The Resident app is intended for adult residents and authorised household users of a housing society. It is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate consent, contact us and we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Material updates may also be communicated in the app, by email, or via your society. Continued use of the app after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.

11. Contact us

Privacy questions

Email: support@jameen.com

For society-specific data questions (unit records, invoices, membership), please also contact your housing society or management company through the channels they provide in the app.
