PARCEL 07 · HELPDESK

Complaints that don't disappear into a WhatsApp thread

A full ticketing system built for housing societies, with SLA timers and escalation — not a generic support desk bolted on.

  • Residents raise tickets by category — plumbing, electrical, security, common area — with photos attached, tracked from open to resolved
  • Configurable SLA clocks for response and resolution; a ticket that breaches SLA escalates automatically up your committee's chain
  • Vendor and asset records tied to every ticket, plus recurring preventive-maintenance schedules so AMC visits are never missed
  • Resident feedback closes the loop, and every asset builds up its own maintenance history over time

Nothing gets marked "resolved" without the resident confirming it — and every escalation is logged, so a vendor at fault is chargeback-ready.

PARCEL 08 · VISITOR MANAGEMENT

Know who's at your gate, before they're at your gate

Security staff and residents work off the same real-time system — no logbooks, no phone calls to confirm a delivery.

  • Residents pre-approve guests, deliveries and cabs from the app; the gate sees the approval instantly
  • Photo capture and instant notification when a visitor arrives, with approve or deny from the resident's phone
  • Digital visitor logs replace paper registers — searchable and exportable for committee security audits
  • Recurring-visitor profiles for staff and daily-help, so regulars aren't re-verified at every entry

Built for the guard's actual workflow at the gate — fast entries — not a resident-only feature that security ends up ignoring.

PARCEL 09 · ONLINE PAYMENTS

Maintenance dues collected, not chased

GST-compliant invoicing, automated reminders, and a full accounting engine behind every rupee collected.

  • Auto-generated, GST-compliant invoices with correct CGST/SGST breakup and FY-aware, race-safe invoice numbering
  • Residents pay online in a few taps; payments reconcile automatically against dues — no manual matching in a spreadsheet
  • Late-payment penalties applied automatically, with credit notes and refunds handled cleanly instead of "adjust it next month"
  • Full subledger-level accounting underneath, so the treasurer's books stay audit-ready year-round

This isn't a payment link bolted onto a spreadsheet — it's real double-entry accounting, defensible at the AGM.
